Using Chatty in Google Meet ↗
  1. Join Meet on this laptop. Have the other participants join from their own devices.
  2. Click Enable audio devices, then select a virtual audio output such as Microsoft Teams Audio Device. In Meet, select that same device as the microphone and unmute it. Keep Meet's speakers on headphones or the normal speakers.
  3. Select Meeting tab, click Start session, choose the Meet tab, and enable Share tab audio.
  4. Say “Chatty” followed by one request. After its answer, Chatty waits for its name again. Say “Chatty, stop” to interrupt. For changes, listen to Chatty’s proposal, then say “yes” to approve or “no” to reject. No browser approval is needed. The silence detector is a practical timing heuristic; use Stop speaking if needed.
  5. To ask about another participant's shared screen, enable incoming screen context before Start. Chatty sends a single current snapshot only for your screen question.

Use Chrome on localhost. Chatty joins through this laptop’s meeting microphone. Resume opens one addressed request; it does not flush earlier generated audio.
